# Yemanjā — Creative Direction

## Objective

Create a website that makes the visitor feel they have discovered a private tropical escape in Dakar and moves them naturally toward reserving a table.

The website must feel specific to Yemanjā: ocean, palms, food, music and art. It must not look like a generic luxury restaurant template.

## Brand signals observed

- Name styling: **Yemanjā**
- Endorsement: **By Sweet Coffee**
- Positioning: **Food. Drinks. Music & Art by the sea**
- Main visual motifs: palms, Atlantic horizon, woven rattan, warm wood, white plaster, daylight and sunset shadows
- Food direction visible so far: seafood, ceviche, mussels, tropical ingredients and refined plating
- Experience cues: escape, paradise, seaside tables, music, events and reservation-led service
- Current public booking number used in Instagram captions: **+221 77 635 65 15**
- Current hours shown in recent captions: **17h–00h** (must be confirmed)

## Brand story confirmed by the printed menu

Yemanjā describes itself as an invitation to travel and a tribute to the sea and its mysteries. The name is inspired by the goddess of the oceans and is associated with femininity, protection and renewal. The experience is explicitly framed around taste, light and emotion meeting by the coast.

The menu repeatedly uses an ammonite-like spiral shell illustration. This should become a secondary visual signature across loading states, section markers and subtle transitions. It complements the palm entrance by connecting the tropical landscape to the marine story.

## Signature entrance: “Beyond the palms”

### Opening frame

The viewport is filled by two layers of detailed palm leaves, one entering from the left and one from the right. They overlap enough to conceal the destination behind them. The leaves move very slightly in a sea breeze.

Centered between them:

**YEMANJĀ**  
*By Sweet Coffee*

Below the mark is a restrained prompt such as **Enter Yemanjā** or **Discover the escape**.

### Interaction

When the visitor clicks or taps:

1. The palm layers separate toward the edges.
2. Sunlight blooms gently through the opening.
3. The logo recedes rather than abruptly disappearing.
4. The main hero is revealed: ocean, architecture and a prepared table.
5. The first reservation call-to-action becomes visible immediately.

The transition should feel like physically walking through tropical foliage into the restaurant.

### Quality guardrails

- Keep the reveal between 1.4 and 2.2 seconds.
- Allow the visitor to skip it.
- Do not replay it on every page or every return visit.
- Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings.
- Never autoplay sound. Offer an optional ambient-sound control later.
- Use optimized layered assets so the entrance remains smooth on ordinary mobile connections.
- The website must still be usable if animation or JavaScript fails.

## Main website journey

1. **Entrance** — palms conceal and reveal the experience.
2. **Hero** — an immediate emotional view and a clear “Reserve a table” action.
3. **The escape** — short editorial copy about Yemanjā by the Atlantic.
4. **Taste** — selected dishes and cocktails; full menu added after Bijou sends it.
5. **Atmosphere** — music, art, golden hour and evening transformation.
6. **Gallery** — immersive images arranged like an editorial travel story, not an Instagram clone.
7. **Events** — curated evenings and special programming.
8. **Reservation** — frictionless WhatsApp/phone flow with date, time and party size.
9. **Visit** — map, opening hours, practical arrival information and social links.

## Visual system

### Palette

- Shell: warm ivory
- Lagoon: muted blue-green
- Deep Atlantic: dark ocean blue
- Palm: dusty green
- Clay: sun-baked terracotta
- Night: near-black blue

### Typography

Use a refined editorial serif for emotional headlines and a clean, understated sans serif for navigation, details and reservation controls. The typography should feel coastal and international without imitating a Balinese hotel brand.

### Motion

Slow organic movement: palm shadows, soft image reveals, drifting light and restrained parallax. Reservation actions must remain fast and direct.

## Working principle

The experience can reference Bali through its calm, tropical hospitality, but the final identity must belong to Dakar and the Atlantic. Yemanjā should feel like a destination that could only exist here.
